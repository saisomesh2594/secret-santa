Secret Santa draw

Add your group, draw once, and send each person only their own match. Everything runs in this page. Nothing leaves it unless you choose automatic email sending.

1 Who’s in

Type or paste one person per line (name, then email), or load a CSV file. Commas, tabs, semicolons and “Name <email>” all work. If your list is in Excel, save it as CSV first.

Keep some people from drawing each other (optional)

Useful for partners or people who share a bench. One group per line, using names or emails as in the list above. Nobody in a line will get anyone else in that line, so a row can hold a whole family.

2 The exchange and the email

These go into each person’s email. Leave anything blank to skip it.
